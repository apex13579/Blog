When I test an issue on my network I like to start by running down the osi model as a guide.
1. The first thing I check is the physical layer so I start by shecking the wiring and testing the pins (a tester is pretty inexpensive and will let you know if one of the pins is the issue)
while looking at the wiring look at the RJ45 ends. I know the convience of the pass through connectors is great but they will sometimes cause a short which can kill not only the cable but the switch port or the switch as a whole.
From there I will inspect the ports as well as cleaning them with canned air as dust and pet hair can cause issues. after that I will inspect the cables themselves.
2. After all the physical layer has been inspected I will then move to the next layer which is the data link layer and as the "heartbeat" of the network the first thing I will do is ping the router/gateway.
