# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We pledge to keep our community respectful, inclusive, and harassment-free for everyone. This applies to all visitors, readers, and members of my blog and associated platforms.

## Our Standards

### Positive Behavior
- Be respectful and kind to others.
- Provide constructive feedback only.
- Engage in discussions that build the community.

### Unacceptable Behavior
- Harassment, insults, or derogatory comments.
- Any form of discrimination or inappropriate content.
- Spamming or disruptive actions.

## Enforcement

Violations will be handled by me personally. Contact me with proof (screenshots required). I'll issue warnings first. After 3 warnings, you'll be banned from the community.

## Contact

Report issues on Discord. Screenshots are required as proof is essential.

---

This Code of Conduct ensures a positive experience for all. Violations may result in removal from the community.
